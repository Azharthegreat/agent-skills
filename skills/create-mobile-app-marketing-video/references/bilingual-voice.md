# Bilingual Voice and Captions

## Urdu

- Write for spoken Pakistani Urdu, using natural everyday vocabulary.
- Use Urdu script by default for final captions and the delivery script.
- Include Roman Urdu only when it helps pronunciation or the user requests it.
- Keep English product names and technical terms only where they sound natural.
- Mark uncertain brand pronunciations before synthesis.

## English

- Adapt the message for the target market instead of translating sentence by sentence.
- Use short, speakable sentences and a clear commercial rhythm.
- Avoid inflated claims and unnatural corporate language.

## Voice direction

Specify language, gender preference, age impression, accent, warmth, confidence, pace, energy, pauses, and pronunciations. Generate a short sample first when voice selection materially affects the campaign.

## Audio mix

- Normalize narration consistently across language versions.
- Duck music under speech and restore it gently between lines.
- Remove clipped peaks, harsh sibilance, hum, and long silence.
- Listen on both headphones and a phone speaker before approval.

## Captions

Keep captions to one or two lines, break on phrases, avoid covering important UI, and preserve safe margins. Check Urdu shaping and right-to-left punctuation in the final render rather than trusting source text alone.
