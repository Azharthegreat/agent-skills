# Render and QA

## Export targets

Use H.264 MP4 with AAC audio unless the destination requires another codec. Keep a high-quality master before aggressive platform compression.

## Visual QA

- Verify source orientation, crop, scale, device frame alignment, and safe areas.
- Confirm every screenshot and recording belongs to the correct app/version.
- Check text spelling, Urdu shaping, English grammar, CTA, logo, and store URL.
- Inspect transitions for flashes, black frames, duplicate frames, and abrupt crops.
- Confirm personal data is removed from every frame, including quick transitions.

## Audio QA

- Confirm narration matches captions and visible actions.
- Check pronunciation, pacing, music ducking, noise, clipping, and final silence.
- Ensure the Urdu and English versions communicate the same verified claims.

## Technical QA

- Verify width, height, duration, frame rate, codec, audio stream, file size, and playback.
- Render representative frame images from opening, first feature, middle, final feature, and CTA.
- Watch the entire final MP4 once at normal speed before delivery.

## Delivery matrix

Track language, aspect ratio, duration, resolution, caption mode, voice version, final filename, and QA status for every output.
